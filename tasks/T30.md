# T30 Emote animations, batch 2: point, facepalm, dance, sit
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T29

## Goal
Four more animations in the same model file, copying the approach that worked in T29.

## Steps
1. Read how T29's animations are built (salute and clap are the best reference).
2. Animations:
   - `point` (1.5 s, once): right arm straight out where the player faces, hold, lower.
   - `facepalm` (2.0 s, once): right hand to the face, head dips, slow lower.
   - `dance` (loops until cancelled): simple 1.6 s loop, arms swing and body sways side to side. Uses the loop flag from T27.
   - `sit` (loops until cancelled): legs forward, body upright. The cancel rules already stop it when the player moves.
3. Same easing rules as T29. Flip the "not animated yet" flags off.
4. Extend the T29 test to the new animations.
