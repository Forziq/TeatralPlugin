# T33 Vehicle health, damage and repairs
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: G3

Phase 4 starts here: tanks done properly.

## Goal
Vehicles can be damaged and destroyed, so tank fights have a point. Block damage stays exactly as it is now (Egor parked that for later).

## Steps
1. Health on `VehicleType`, hard-coded: tank 100, jeep 40.
2. Damage: a shell hitting a vehicle does 25 to the front, 35 to the side and 50 to the back of a tank (work out which face was hit from the shell direction and the hull yaw). Jeeps take 40 from any side. Splash from explosions nearby does a small amount. Players' melee and arrows do nothing to tanks, a little to jeeps.
3. Destroyed: big explosion effect at the vehicle (no extra block damage beyond today's shell explosion), crew thrown out and hurt, vehicle removed. The pad can then spawn a new one.
4. Below 30%: smoke particles from the hull.
5. Repairs: right-click a vehicle from outside with an iron ingot to restore 10% (uses the ingot). Full health says so.
6. Pure classes for the hit-side maths and health, with unit tests (front/side/back from a few angles, can't go below 0 or above max).
7. Messages in both lang files.
