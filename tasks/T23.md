# T23 Server radio: /forzammilmod music
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T22, T05

## Goal
A shared radio for the whole server. Anyone can control it, and everyone online hears the same track.

## Commands (all under /forzammilmod, tab completion on every argument)
- `/forzammilmod music play <track>`: plays that track for everyone, then stops (unless loop is on).
- `/forzammilmod music stop`: stops the music for everyone and turns loop and shuffle off.
- `/forzammilmod music shuffle`: plays all tracks in random order without repeats until each has played, then reshuffles, and keeps going until stopped.
- `/forzammilmod music loop`: toggles repeating the current track. If nothing is playing, it says so.
- `/forzammilmod music list`: lists tracks with their names in the player's language, marking the one playing.

Track ids come from `assets/music/tracks.yml` (copied into the jar as `music/tracks.yml` at build). Unknown ids get a translated "no such track" message plus the list.

## How it works
1. `RadioService` holds the state: current track, start time, loop on/off, shuffle on/off, the shuffle queue.
2. Playing: for every online player, stop any previous radio sound, then `player.playSound(player, "forzammilmod:music.<id>", SoundCategory.RECORDS, 1f, 1f)`. RECORDS means it follows the "Jukebox/Note Blocks" volume slider, so anyone can turn it down without touching other sounds.
3. Stopping: `player.stopSound("forzammilmod:music.<id>", SoundCategory.RECORDS)` for everyone.
4. Track end: schedule a task for the track's length (from tracks.yml) plus one second. On end: loop replays the same track, shuffle moves to the next, otherwise stop. Cancel and reschedule cleanly on every command so timers never overlap.
5. Players who join mid-track can't hear it from the middle (Minecraft can't start a sound part way through). Tell them in chat what's playing and that they'll hear from the next track. In loop or shuffle mode they join in at the next start.
6. Everyone gets a short translated chat line when the music changes, e.g. "Egor put on «Стальной марш»" for Russian players and "Egor put on Stalnoy Marsh" for English ones. Players type the Latin ids, e.g. `/forzammilmod music play stalnoy_marsh`.
7. Stop the radio on plugin disable.
8. Pure classes for the shuffle queue and the "what happens when a track ends" logic, with unit tests (no repeats until the queue empties, loop beats shuffle, stop clears both).
