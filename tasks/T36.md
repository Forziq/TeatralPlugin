# T36 Vehicle sounds
size: M | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T34

## Goal
Vehicles use the sounds already made in T04 instead of vanilla ones. No new ElevenLabs runs.

## Steps
1. Tank: `tank_engine_idle` loops while someone's in it; `tank_engine_rev` when throttle goes from stopped to moving; `tank_tracks_loop` while moving; `turret_traverse` when the turret turns fast (throttled, not every tick); `cannon_fire` on firing; `shell_whistle` near the shell's path; `explosion_small` on impact; `reload_clank` when reload finishes; `armour_hit` when a shell hits a vehicle (T33).
2. Jeep: `jeep_engine_loop` while someone's in it; `jeep_horn` when the driver presses jump.
3. Loops: Minecraft can't loop sounds itself, so replay each loop just before it ends (lengths in assets/sounds/README.md), and stop it cleanly when the vehicle stops, empties or is destroyed. One loop per vehicle per sound, heard within about 32 blocks.
4. Use a vehicle-friendly sound category (`NEUTRAL` or `BLOCKS`) so it isn't tied to the music slider.
5. Pure class for loop timing (when to replay, when to stop), with unit tests.
