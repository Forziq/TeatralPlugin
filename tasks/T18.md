# T18 Firing, shells and explosions
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T17

## Goal
The tank driver clicks to fire a shell that flies in a straight line and explodes on impact.

## Steps
1. Detect the driver's click while riding (method from docs/notes/vehicles.md).
2. Shell: starts at the muzzle, moves 3 blocks per tick in a straight line (step the ray so it can't skip through thin walls), max range 150 blocks. Show it with a small particle trail.
3. On hitting a block or entity: `world.createExplosion(location, 2.5f, false, true, driver)` so blocks break and players get hurt, credited to the driver. The driver's own tank isn't damaged (no health yet).
4. Reload: 4 seconds, hard-coded. Show remaining time on the action bar using the plural message (`reload.seconds`).
5. Vanilla sounds only for now (`ENTITY_GENERIC_EXPLODE`, a cannon-like vanilla sound for firing). Our ElevenLabs sounds wait for Phase 4, as the brief says.
6. Unit tests for the ray stepping and reload timer.
