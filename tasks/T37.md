# T37 Vehicle HUD
size: M | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T36

## Goal
Whoever's in a vehicle can see its state at a glance.

## Steps
1. Boss bar for everyone inside: vehicle name and health (colour goes green, yellow, red).
2. Action bar: driver sees speed; whoever fires sees "Ready" or the reload countdown (keep the existing plural message).
3. Bars appear on getting in and disappear on getting out, death, quit or the vehicle being destroyed.
4. Translated text in both lang files.
