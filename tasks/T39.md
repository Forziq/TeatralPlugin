# T39 Army truck
size: M | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: G4

Phase 5 starts here: more vehicles on the same code.

## Goal
A WWII army truck (ZIS-5 style) that carries a squad, spawned from a pad with `fmod vehicle spawn truck`.

## Steps
1. `VehicleType.TRUCK`: slower than the jeep, faster than the tank, wide turning circle, health 60, no gun. Add a `truck` model in the same style as T35 with a driver seat, one cab passenger and six seats in the covered back, wheels that spin.
2. Reuse the jeep's driving, sounds (jeep engine pitched lower) and HUD.
3. Translated name in both lang files. Tab completion and pads pick it up automatically.
4. Extend the stats and model tests.
