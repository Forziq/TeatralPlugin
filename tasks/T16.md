# T16 Driving core
size: L | who: opus-deep | hard: yes | deps: T15

Hard because the feel of the whole vehicle side depends on this, and lag and jitter are the brief's biggest risk.

## Goal
Egor spawns a tank with a command, anyone can climb in and drive it with W/A/S/D, and it moves smoothly and climbs one block.

## Steps
1. Read docs/notes/vehicles.md.
2. Design a `Vehicle` base (state, tick, despawn) and `VehicleType` (tank, jeep) with hard-coded stats: max speed, acceleration, braking, turn rate, step height. Tank: slow and heavy. Keep physics in pure classes (`DriveModel`: input + state + dt gives new position/yaw) so they can be unit tested without a server.
3. `/fmod vehicle spawn tank|jeep` (owner only) spawns at the block the owner is looking at. `/fmod vehicle remove` removes the nearest within 10 blocks. `/fmod vehicle list` shows active ones. Vehicles aren't saved yet; remove them all on disable.
4. Mounting: right-click the vehicle to get in as driver, sneak to get out. Only one driver.
5. Input from `PlayerInputEvent` / `getCurrentInput()`. W/S accelerate/brake/reverse, A/D turn the hull (tanks can turn on the spot).
6. Movement each tick: gravity, ground check under the footprint, step up one block when blocked by a one-block wall, stop at taller walls, don't fall through or get stuck inside blocks. Use whatever interpolation the notes recommend so other players don't see it jitter.
7. Driver camera stays attached and free to look around.
8. Unit tests for `DriveModel`: acceleration caps at max speed, turning rate, braking, step-up decision.
9. Translated messages for every command reply.
