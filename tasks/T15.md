# T15 Tank and jeep placeholder models
size: M | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T14

## Goal
Two rough .bbmodel files the vehicle code can drive.

## Steps
1. `models/tank.bbmodel`: about 3 blocks wide, 5 long, 2 high. Bones `hull`, `turret` (child of hull, pivot at the turret ring), `barrel` (child of turret, pivot at the barrel base). A locator `muzzle` at the barrel tip and `seat` where the driver sits. Plain flat colours (olive green, dark grey tracks), simple cubes, no textures beyond solid colour, no animations.
2. `models/jeep.bbmodel`: about 2 wide, 3.5 long. Bones `body`, `wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr` (separate so they can spin later), locator `seat`.
3. Follow whatever naming and scale rules docs/notes/vehicles.md and docs/notes/bettermodel.md give.
4. Unit test parses both files and checks the required bones and locators exist.
5. Add to manual.md M05 (optional): open both in Blockbench to eyeball them.

## Done when
Smoke test shows BetterModel loaded both models without errors.
