# T40 Field artillery
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T39

## Goal
A field gun (ZiS-3 style) spawned from a pad with `fmod vehicle spawn artillery`. It doesn't drive; it lobs shells in an arc over long distances.

## Steps
1. `VehicleType.ARTILLERY`: can't move under its own power (W/S do nothing), A/D turn the whole gun slowly. Health 50. Model in the T35 style with a gunner seat behind the shield and a barrel bone.
2. Aim: the barrel follows the gunner's look, pitch from 0 to 45 degrees.
3. Shells follow a ballistic arc (gravity each tick), range up to about 300 blocks at 45 degrees, stepped like T18's ray so they can't pass through walls. Explosion the same size as the tank's for now (block damage stays parked). 8 s reload.
4. `shell_whistle` as the shell comes down near players; `cannon_fire` on firing.
5. Pure class for the arc maths with unit tests (range at a few angles, never skips a block).
6. Translated name and messages.
