# T29 Emote animations, batch 1: salute, clap, cheer, bow
size: L | who: opus-deep | hard: yes | deps: T27

Hard because the keyframes go straight into the player model JSON and nobody sees them until Egor joins. The brief warns AI keyframes look stiff, so take care with timing and easing.

## Goal
Four new animations in `models/players/forzammil_player.bbmodel`, built the same way as `wave` (T11).

## Steps
1. Read docs/notes/bettermodel.md and how `wave` is built in the model file.
2. Animations, all play once:
   - `salute` (2.0 s): right hand to the brow, hold, snap down. Body straight.
   - `clap` (2.0 s): both arms forward, four claps, slight body bob. Sound `emote_clap`.
   - `cheer` (2.5 s): both arms up twice, small jump in the body bone if the rig allows. Sound `emote_cheer`.
   - `bow` (2.0 s): bend forward from the body about 45 degrees, hold, rise.
3. Ease in and out on every keyframe, slight overshoot on fast moves so they don't look robotic. Keep the legs planted unless the move needs them.
4. Flip their "not animated yet" flag off in the emote list (T27).
5. Unit test: the JSON parses, each animation exists with the right length, and only known bones are animated.
