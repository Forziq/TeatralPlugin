# T48 Help, guide and tidy-up
size: M | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T47

## Goal
Friends can work everything out without asking Egor, and the repo is tidy for the 1.0 release.

## Steps
1. `/fmod help` lists only the commands the player can use, translated, with one line each. `/fmod` alone shows the same.
2. A short in-game guide on first join (once per player, remembered in a small file in the plugin's data folder): emotes menu, pads, controls, the radio.
3. `docs/guide.md` in English and `docs/guide.ru.md` in Russian for Egor to share: emotes, vehicles and controls, pads, repairs, music.
4. Read through every line in `lang/ru.yml` for natural Russian and consistent wording (one term for tank, gunner, pad, and so on).
5. Tidy manual.md: move finished one-off steps into a "Done" section at the bottom.
