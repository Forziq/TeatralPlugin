# T34 Crew seats: gunner and passengers
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T33

## Goal
A tank can carry a driver and a gunner, and the jeep can carry passengers. Read docs/notes/vehicles.md first: seat bones use the `p_` (driver) and `sp_` (passenger) tags, and BetterModel strips the tag from the name.

## Steps
1. Tank: add a `sp_gunner` seat bone in the turret. Jeep: add `sp_passenger1` and `sp_passenger2`. Keep the placeholder look; only add the seat bones.
2. Right-click: first free seat in order driver, gunner, passengers. Sneak gets out.
3. Tank with a gunner: the gunner aims the turret and fires; the driver only drives. With no gunner, the driver does both, as now.
4. Passengers just ride and can look around.
5. When the vehicle is destroyed (T33), everyone inside is thrown out.
6. Pure class for "who controls the turret" and seat order, with unit tests.
7. Messages in both lang files (e.g. "You're the gunner. Look to aim, click to fire.").
