# T47 Performance pass
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T46

## Goal
The server stays at 20 TPS with a busy fight: 10 vehicles, several firing, music playing, people emoting.

## Steps
1. sonnet-tester writes a load script for the local server: spawn 10 vehicles and fire shells on a timer (a hidden owner-only `/fmod debug load` is fine), then reads `/tps` and the timings for our plugin.
2. Fix the biggest costs it finds. Likely places: per-tick work for parked, empty vehicles (sleep them), shells checked every tick, sound loops, repeated bone lookups, boss bar updates every tick (throttle to a few times a second).
3. Hard caps where it helps: live shells at once, explosion particles.
4. Write the before and after numbers in progress.md.
