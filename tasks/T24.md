# T24 Swap two tracks for tragic, intense ones
size: M | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T22

Runs straight away because Egor's ElevenLabs plan runs out soon.

## Goal
`polevaya_kukhnya` and `tankovy_proryv` are gone completely, and two new instrumentals that are much more tragic and intense take their place.

## Remove
Delete every trace of `polevaya_kukhnya` and `tankovy_proryv`: the `.ogg` files in `assets/music/` (and `raw/`, `alt/` if present), their entries in `assets/music/tracks.yml`, `assets/pack/assets/forzammilmod/sounds.json` and `assets/music/README.md`, and any mention in code, lang files or tests (`grep -rn` for both ids).

## New tracks
Same rules as T22: names are Russian written in Latin letters for ids and English-language players, Cyrillic for Russian players. Never name or imitate a real song, composer or film score in a prompt. No sung words; a wordless choir is fine.

| id | Name for English players | Name for Russian players | Prompt idea | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `posledniy_rubezh` | Posledniy Rubezh | Последний рубеж | desperate last stand, epic tragic orchestral, pounding war drums, low brass, wordless male choir, relentless build to a devastating climax, no lyrics | about 2:00 |
| `pepel_goroda` | Pepel Goroda | Пепел города | ruined city after the battle, slow grieving solo cello and strings, distant bells, swelling to an intense heartbreaking crescendo then fading to silence, no lyrics | about 2:30 |

## Steps
1. Check credits left. One take each; regenerate once only if a take is clearly wrong (not tragic, has words, silence).
2. Generate both through the same ElevenLabs music route T22 used (see assets/music/README.md).
3. Convert exactly like T22: stereo Ogg Vorbis 44.1 kHz, loudness normalised to match the other tracks, silence trimmed.
4. Add both to tracks.yml (with `length_ms` from ffprobe, `vocals: false`), sounds.json (`forzammilmod:music.<id>`, `"stream": true`) and the README table with the prompt and credits used.
5. Dry-run pack build and check neither old track is in the zip and both new ones are.

## Done when
tracks.yml has stalnoy_marsh, okopny_vals, posledniy_rubezh, nochnoy_konvoy, pepel_goroda, okopnye_chastushki and pesnya_pro_adama, and nothing anywhere mentions the two removed ids.
