# T31 Emote menu
size: M | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T50

## Goal
`/emote` on its own opens a small chest menu with every emote. Clicking one plays it. `/emote <id>` still works as a shortcut.

## Steps
1. Two-row (18-slot) inventory menu (ten emotes with the greeting, room for more), title translated. Each emote shows as its icon item from the emote list, with its translated name and "loops until you move" in the lore for looping ones.
2. Clicking plays the emote and closes the menu. While on cooldown, the item shows the seconds left and clicking plays `ui_error`; otherwise `ui_click`.
3. Players can't take items out or shift-click anything in. Cancel every click and drag in the menu.
4. Menu text in both lang files.
5. Pure class for the slot layout (which emote goes where) with a unit test.
