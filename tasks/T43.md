# T43 Rough plane
size: L | who: opus-deep | hard: yes | deps: T42

Hard because flight is the riskiest part of the whole project (the brief says so) and the feel decides whether planes survive.

## Goal
One rough fighter plane, cheap to throw away, to answer one question at Gate 6: is flying fun enough on our server?

## Steps
1. Read docs/notes/planes.md and docs/notes/vehicles.md.
2. `VehicleType.PLANE`, placeholder model (fuselage, wings, propeller bone that spins), pilot seat. Spawned from a pad like everything else.
3. Flight in a pure `FlightModel`: throttle (W/S), roll and turn (A/D), pitch from where the pilot looks. Takes off above a set speed, stalls and drops when too slow, lands when touching the ground slowly, crashes and explodes when hitting the ground or a wall fast.
4. `plane_engine_loop` while flying, `plane_flyby` for players it passes close to.
5. No weapons, health or HUD beyond speed and height on the action bar.
6. Unit tests for `FlightModel`: take-off speed, stall, crash versus landing.
7. Whatever manual steps the research found (e.g. `allow-flight`) go into manual.md.
