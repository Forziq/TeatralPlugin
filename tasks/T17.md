# T17 Turret and barrel aim
size: L | who: sonnet-builder | hard: no | deps: T16

## Goal
The tank's turret turns towards where the driver looks and the barrel tilts within limits, at a believable speed.

## Steps
1. Each tick, target turret yaw = driver's head yaw relative to the hull. Turn the `turret` bone towards it at max 40 degrees per second (hard-coded), taking the shortest way round.
2. Barrel pitch follows the driver's pitch, clamped to -5 (down) .. +20 (up) degrees, max 25 degrees per second.
3. Pure `TurretModel` class for the maths with unit tests: wrap-around at 180/-180, clamping, rate limiting.
4. Use the `muzzle` locator (or computed bone transform) to get the barrel tip position and direction; expose it for T18.
