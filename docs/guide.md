# forzammilmod: player guide

Emotes, wartime vehicles and a shared radio. Everything below works for everyone unless it says otherwise. In game, `/fmod help` lists the commands you can use, and `/fmod guide` shows a short version of this page.

## Emotes
- `/emote wave` waves at everyone. `/emote greeting` plays the greeting. Type `/emote` and press Tab to see what is available.
- Move, or type `/emote stop`, to end an emote.
- There is a short wait between emotes (the game tells you how long).

## Getting a vehicle
Look for a **pad**: a command block with a button next to it. Press the button and the vehicle appears 2 blocks above the pad, so keep that space clear.
- If one is already standing on the pad, the pad says so and does nothing.
- There is a limit on how many vehicles can be out at once (10 unless the owner changed it). A vehicle nobody drives for a while (10 minutes unless changed) disappears.
- `/fmod vehicle list` shows where vehicles are. Only the owner can spawn or remove vehicles by typing a command.

## Getting in and out
Right-click a vehicle to sit in the first free seat. Sneak to get out.

| Vehicle | Seats |
|---|---|
| Jeep, armoured jeep | driver + 2 passengers |
| Army truck | driver + 7 passengers, no gun |
| Tank, heavy tank | driver + gunner |
| Field gun | one gunner |
| Fighter plane | one pilot |

## Driving
- **Jeep and truck:** W/S drive and reverse, A/D steer (they only turn while moving). Space honks the horn.
- **Tank:** W/S drive, A/D turn (it can turn on the spot). The gunner looks to aim the turret and left-clicks to fire; reload takes 4 seconds. With no gunner, the driver aims and fires too.
- **Field gun:** it can't drive. A/D swing the whole gun slowly, looking up and down raises the barrel, left-click fires. Reload takes 8 seconds.
- **Plane:** W/S open and close the throttle, A/D bank and turn, hold Space to climb, hold Sprint (Ctrl) to dive. Let go of both and the nose levels out. Take off by getting up to speed, then holding Space. Left-click fires the machine guns in short bursts. Sneak to bail out: high enough up, you get a parachute. For a safe landing come in slow and shallow.

## Repairs
Hold an **iron ingot** and right-click the vehicle from outside. Each ingot restores 10% of its health. If it is already fine, nothing is used.

## Music radio
One radio for the whole server, so everyone hears the same thing.
- `/fmod music list` shows the tracks.
- `/fmod music play <track>` starts one, `stop` turns it off.
- `shuffle` plays tracks in random order, `loop` repeats the current one.

## Resource pack
The server needs its resource pack, so accept it when asked (declining kicks you). `/fmod pack` checks that it loaded and plays a test sound.
